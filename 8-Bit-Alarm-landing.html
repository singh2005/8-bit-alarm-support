The gamified anti-snooze alarm clock that forces your mind awake!

Why 8-Bit Alarm?

Tired of accidentally hitting snooze on standard alarm clocks and falling back asleep? 8-Bit Alarm gamifies your morning routine with nostalgic retro sound vibes and interactive swipe challenges that engage your brain right as you wake up.

Key Features:

  • Easy Mode: Swipe in 3 random directions to turn off the alarm. Perfect for light sleepers.
  • Hard Mode: Register 6 random directional swipes in under 4 seconds or the timer resets!
  • Retro 8-Bit Aesthetic: Clean, pixel-art design inspired by classic arcade games.
  • 100% Offline & Private: No backend, no accounts, and no data tracking. Works reliably anywhere.
