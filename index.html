Frequently Asked Questions

Does the alarm sound in Airplane Mode or Offline?

Yes. 8-Bit Alarm schedules notifications and audio locally on your device. No internet connection or cellular signal is required.

How do I stop or snooze an active alarm?

Depending on your configured setting (Easy or Hard), you must complete the random directional swipe gesture challenge on screen to turn off the alarm.

How do I revoke permissions?

You can manage or disable Notification permissions at any time via your device settings: Settings > 8-Bit Alarm > Notifications.
