Privacy Policy for 8-Bit Alarm

Effective Date: August 14, 2026

Summary: 8-Bit Alarm is designed as an offline-first, standalone local utility. We do not collect, process, track, or share any personal data from our users.

1. Data Collection & Processing

We do not collect any personal information or device data. The app operates 100% locally on your smartphone or tablet. Specifically:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or user IDs.
  • No Location Tracking: The app does not access, record, or store location data.
  • No Media/File Access: The app does not access your personal photos, files, or contacts.
  • No Analytics or Ads: There are no third-party analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, or tracking software built into the app.

2. Permissions Requested

To perform its core utility functions, 8-Bit Alarm requests the following system permissions:

  • Notifications: Required to trigger local audio alerts and display the alarm UI when an alarm goes off. Notification triggers remain local to your device and are never sent to external servers.

3. Revoking Consent & Managing Permissions

You can revoke system permissions or stop local data storage at any time:

  • Notifications: Disable via your device's Settings > Notifications > 8-Bit Alarm.
  • App Removal: Deleting the 8-Bit Alarm app from your device immediately wipes all locally stored alarm schedules and configuration preferences.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Because we do not collect or store user data on external servers, there is no remote user data to retain or delete. All user-configured alarm settings reside purely in local application memory and are permanently removed when the application is uninstalled.

5. Children’s Privacy

8-Bit Alarm complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR/Google Play Families policies. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the EU/UK).
